# Support

For a reproducible product bug or feature request, use the relevant repository's issues when enabled. Include the product version, expected behavior and steps to reproduce. Remove credentials and personal or customer data from any logs or screenshots.

For account, billing or business enquiries, contact [info@codarasolutions.com](mailto:info@codarasolutions.com). Do not post account or payment details in public issues.

For vulnerabilities, follow the [security policy](https://github.com/Codara-Solutions/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Repository-specific support instructions take precedence.
